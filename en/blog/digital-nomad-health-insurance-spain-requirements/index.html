Digital Nomad Health Insurance Spain 2026: UGE & Telework Visa Requirements

24 August 2026 8 min read Verified content

The Spanish Digital Nomad Visa (created under the pioneering Startup Act / Ley de Startups 28/2022) has transformed Spain into Europe's leading hub for international tech professionals, remote employees, and freelancers. Whether applying at a Spanish Consulate in your home country (1-year visa) or directly within Spain before the Large Business and Strategic Collectives Unit (UGE-CE) for a 3-year residence permit, private health insurance is one of the most strictly evaluated legal requirements.

Spanish immigration authorities will issue a Requerimiento de Subsanación (formal notice of defect) if your insurance policy includes copayments, deductibles, or reimbursement limits. In this official guide by VitaBlue, we examine the mandatory conditions your private health insurance must meet to guarantee approval.

The 4 Mandatory Health Insurance Pillars for the Digital Nomad Visa

To be accepted by both the UGE-CE in Madrid and Spanish Consulates abroad, your private health insurance certificate must explicitly certify the following four standards:

  • ✓Licensed Insurer in Spain (DGSFP): The insurance company must be registered with the Directorate-General for Insurance (DGSFP) and operate directly within the Spanish healthcare system (such as ASISA, Sanitas, or Adeslas).
  • ✓Zero Copays & Zero Deductibles (Sin Copagos): You cannot have any out-of-pocket costs per doctor visit, specialist consultation, diagnostic test, or emergency intervention.
  • ✓No Waiting Periods (Sin Carencias): Full access to primary care, high-resolution diagnostic imaging, surgery, and unlimited hospitalization from day one.
  • ✓Sanitary Repatriation & Remains Evacuation: Comprehensive coverage for medical transport or repatriation of mortal remains to your home country in the event of death or severe illness.
ℹ️⚠️ Important Warning: International travel insurance policies (e.g. SafetyWing, World Nomads, Allianz Travel) or US health insurance plans with international riders are systematically rejected by Spanish immigration officers because they operate under reimbursement limits rather than direct hospital admission.

Top Health Insurance Providers for Digital Nomads in Spain

Based on immigration approval rates and digital expat support, the two leading options are:

  • ✓ASISA Internacional: The most cost-effective full-coverage policy for remote workers (from €35/month). Includes HLA hospital network access, unlimited GP visits, and 24h certified English/Spanish consular documentation.
  • ✓Sanitas Más Salud / International: Features the award-winning Blua telemedicine app with English-speaking doctors, digital prescriptions valid across Spanish pharmacies, and immediate PDF certificate generation.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • ✓Can I include my spouse and children under the same policy?: Yes. Family members can be added to the same contract, each receiving an individual certificate for their dependent visa.
  • ✓How do video consultations work for remote workers?: Both ASISA and Sanitas offer digital apps with 24/7 video appointments in English and electronic prescriptions accepted at any Spanish pharmacy.
  • ✓What if I transition to local Spanish employment later?: If you obtain local Social Security, you can switch or adjust your private health plan seamlessly without losing policy seniority.
