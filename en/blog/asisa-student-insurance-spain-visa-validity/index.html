If you are preparing your application for a Spanish Student Visa (for university degree, master’s program, Erasmus exchange, or Spanish language academy), securing the correct private health insurance is often the most stressful hurdle. Spanish Consulates worldwide (from London, New York, and Miami to New Delhi, Lagos, and Manila) strictly reject travel insurance policies or foreign health plans with copayments.

When browsing through approved Spanish insurance providers, international students frequently encounter ASISA with prices starting around €38 per month—substantially more affordable than Sanitas (€55/mo) or international expat policies (€80+/mo). This raises a crucial question: "Is ASISA actually accepted by the Spanish Consulate and Immigration Office (Extranjería)?"

The short answer is YES. 100% valid and certified. ASISA is one of Spain’s largest and oldest health insurance entities, officially registered under DGSFP code C-0461. Through VitaBlue, we issue hundreds of ASISA consular certificates every month with a 100% acceptance rate. Below is the technical breakdown of how ASISA compares to Sanitas and Adeslas.

When is Sanitas or Adeslas Worth Considering?

While ASISA offers the most competitive price, other providers offer specific features:

  • ✓Sanitas International Students: Ideal for students who prioritize a native English-language mobile app ("Mi Sanitas") and round-the-clock English telemedicine consultations, albeit at a higher premium (~€55/month).
  • ✓Adeslas for Students: Recommended if you are attending university in smaller rural Spanish towns where Quirónsalud or local Adeslas clinics have greater hospital presence than HLA.
ℹ️📌 Repatriation Standard: Both ASISA Health Students (with worldwide travel assistance up to €25,000 including medical transport) and Sanitas International Students fulfill the strict repatriation and emergency transport requirement demanded by Spanish foreign missions.
